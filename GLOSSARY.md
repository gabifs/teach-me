# Teach Me Glossary

An app where a learner studies a topic over many conversations with an AI teacher, which decides what to teach next from what the learner has shown they know.

## Terms

**Learner**:
The person being taught.
_Avoid_: User, student

**Teacher**:
The agent the learner converses with. It interviews, teaches, answers questions, and judges what to teach next.
_Avoid_: Bot, tutor, assistant

**Workspace**:
Everything belonging to one learner's pursuit of one Mission: its Lessons, Learning Records, Resources, and its continuous conversation with the Teacher. A learner can have many.
_Avoid_: Course, topic, project

**Mission**:
The learner's concrete real-world reason for learning something. Every teaching decision traces back to it. With the learner's confirmation it can shift, or expand once achieved (from playing one song on guitar to fingerstyle), and what was learned under the earlier Mission stays available to later Lessons.
_Avoid_: Goal, objective

**Lesson**:
One short, self-contained piece of teaching on a single tightly-scoped thing, tied to the Mission and giving one tangible win.
_Avoid_: Module, chapter, explainer

**Exercise**:
Something in a Lesson the learner answers, once, as evidence of what they know. Either a Quiz or an Open Question.
_Avoid_: Test, assessment

**Quiz**:
An Exercise where the learner picks the one correct answer from several choices.
_Avoid_: Multiple choice question

**Open Question**:
An Exercise where the learner writes their own answer and the Teacher judges it.
_Avoid_: Discursive question, essay

**Learning Record**:
A decision-grade note of something non-obvious about what the learner knows: understanding demonstrated, prior knowledge disclosed, misconception corrected, or Mission shifted. Coverage alone never earns one.
_Avoid_: Progress entry, log

**Zone of proximal development**:
What the learner is ready to be challenged by next, derived from their Mission and Learning Records.
_Avoid_: Level, difficulty

**Resource**:
A curated, annotated, high-trust source. Knowledge Resources ground Lessons; Wisdom Resources are communities to practise with.
_Avoid_: Link, reference

## Flagged ambiguities

**Study plan**: not a concept here. There is no upfront outline of Lessons; only a Mission and the next Lesson, chosen one at a time.
